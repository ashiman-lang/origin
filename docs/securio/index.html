Securio Weekly Dashboard

Weekly cohort view for your latest customer and payment exports.

This version keeps the look and density of the screenshot, but only uses metrics available in the provided CSVs. Revenue comes from the customer export's PLN totals and is converted to USD for the dashboard. Ad spend is shown in USD using the manually entered May 9 and May 10 screenshot amounts, and milestone ROAS uses benchmark-based forecasts for future renewals.

Weekly cohort table

Weekly cohorts with live spend, paid customer counts, current revenue, and projected milestone ROAS.

Cohort Ad Spend USD Paid CPA D0 Rev Net Rev To Date Refunds Dispute Losses D0 ROAS ROAS To Date M1 M2 M3 M6 M9 M12
Revenue values come from the customer export columns `Total Spend`, `Refunded Volume`, and `Dispute Losses`, treated as PLN and converted to USD with `PLN_TO_USD_RATE = 0.26`. Ad spend uses uploaded Meta rows in PLN and converts them to USD. Milestone ROAS uses your retention benchmark table and real offer renewal prices in USD. Later retention is projected from the last actual retention point using the benchmark ratio, projected renewals are rounded to whole users, and once a projected tail hits `1` two times in a row the cohort is treated as dead and all later projected renewals go to `0`. Projected future revenue also uses a 15% haircut.
